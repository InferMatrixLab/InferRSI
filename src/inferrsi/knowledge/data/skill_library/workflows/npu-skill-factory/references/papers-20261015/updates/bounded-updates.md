**Proposed insertion, if absent:**

Separate file-local habit, subsystem invariant, and repository-wide convention. Revise or retire a lesson when later accepted evidence contradicts it. No new history replay or benchmark is authorized here.

## E-U3 — Consolidation proposes; validation decides

**Target:** `src/inferrsi/data/skill_library/methods/skill-evolution-playbook/SKILL.md` and existing evolution references.

**Increment:** distinguish evidence-pool consolidation from incumbent replacement using [Trace2Skill](../references/trace2skill.md), [SkillOpt](../references/skillopt.md), and [E-C3](../cases/methodology-counterexamples.md#e-c3--fixing-targeted-failures-can-break-unrelated-successes).

**Proposed insertion, if absent:**

> Diagnose the causal failure before proposing a corrective rule. Keep successful behaviors that a correction must preserve. Consolidate overlapping evidence into one coherent candidate, but do not treat consensus as an acceptance test. Bound the target passage and behavioral scope of each amendment, inspect its actual size, and retain the incumbent for rollback. Evaluate combined effects and unrelated-success regressions; keep the final test partition outside selection. A tie or uncertain result must not be reported as improvement.

**Expected effect:** less anecdotal rule growth and fewer unobserved interactions. **Regression risk:** narrow edits may fail to realize complementary changes. **Acceptance evidence needed:** full incumbent, evidence lineage, candidate diff, independent evaluation contract, regression observations and rollback state. No automatic thresholds, worker counts, optimizer schedule, or software changes are adopted from the papers.
