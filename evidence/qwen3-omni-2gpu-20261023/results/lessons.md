# Campaign lessons and limits

These observations belong to [acceptance issue #112](https://github.com/InferMatrixLab/InferRSI/issues/112). They do not establish target attainment or a recommended deployment.

- **Freeze the task, not just the input strings.** A full Omni assistant may answer a sentence instead of reading it. The campaign's [readback wrapper](../plan/benchmark_readback.py) adds an explicit readback instruction while preserving original Seed-TTS references. This qualifies text-to-speech readback through the full pipeline; it does not qualify audio-input understanding or free conversation.
- **Fix measurement before optimizing.** Empty audio events must not establish TTFA ([engine PR #9278](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-omni/pull/9278)); one-second request buckets do not measure simultaneous concurrency ([PR #9279](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-omni/pull/9279)). Raw ordered request metrics allow independent aggregate checks. Throughput here counts 24 kHz PCM sample frames, not codec tokens.
- **Warm representative workloads, then observe residual compilation.** The [MRv2](mrv2-run-manifest.json), [KV](predictor-kv-run-manifest.json) and [short-KV](predictor-short-kv-run-manifest.json) screens retained inference-time JIT events. More repetitions of the first prompt alone do not cover all shapes. [Protocol v5](../plan/protocol-v5.json) therefore conditions all concurrency points and freezes whole-pair retry rules. Verbose observation required a separate configuration-forwarding fix ([PR #9281](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-omni/pull/9281)). Monitor coverage remains limited to supported hooks.
- **A reservation does not prove exclusive execution.** A foreign allocation was observed immediately after the [MPS screen](mrv2-mps-run-manifest.json). Its overlap with measured requests was not timestamped, so that screen cannot establish isolation or an MPS speedup. Subsequent executors continuously record owned and foreign CUDA processes and reject isolation failures.
- **Do not promote a faster-looking bundle without quality.** The [full CUDA bundle](full-cuda-run-manifest.json) retained all 256 quality outputs and produced mean WER 0.07514 versus historical baseline 0.03833. It failed that quality screen and missed both RTF targets. The existing short-KV, sampling and convolution options are credited to [upstream PR #8223](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-omni/pull/8223); this combined screen does not identify which mechanism caused the difference. No claim about other hardware or workloads follows.
- **Profile time sums are not recoverable latency.** GPU kernels overlap across stages, and host synchronization often overlaps active GPU work. Use interval unions and request-level measurements rather than adding kernel durations or treating every synchronization interval as idle time. The retained [baseline profile](baseline-profile-summary.json) and [full-bundle trace manifests](full-cuda-run-manifest.json) are diagnostic observations under profiler overhead, not acceptance benchmarks.

The [source table](../plan/source-attribution.md) distinguishes existing mechanisms, deployment adaptations and new measurement fixes. Failed and inconclusive attempts remain part of the evidence; they are not removed from the search history.
