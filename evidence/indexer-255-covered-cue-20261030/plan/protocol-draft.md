## Unchanged manual extraction rule
Select verbatim OPERATION, OBJECT and ALL CONSTRAINTS spans from the authored raw task. Join with `; ` in that order. Preserve uncertainty, environment/API identity, thresholds, negation, safety and requested deliverable. Remove only greetings, politeness, redundant framing or incidental chronology not used by the task; record every removed span and its permitted reason. No added keywords, synonyms, inflection, translation, schema-keywords or answer terms. Do not concatenate separately selected words to invent a new phrase. This is deterministic serialization of manually selected spans, not an automatic semantic extractor.

Do not artificially pad tasks or bait substring matches. Include at least two naturally concise controls among six, with at least one raw/cue byte-identical. If all meaningful wording must be kept, keep it. Record task-author effort, character/whitespace-token counts and whether the only change was polite framing; do not claim broad narrative normalization if that is all this set tests.
